Plants

Every plant has its own data, dashboard layout and people. A person can belong to several plants with a different role in each. Only super-admins see this page.

Your plants
Add a plant
“Start from” copies another plant’s setup (Data Entry columns and departments, dashboard layout and panel settings). It never copies entered numbers, announcements or people. You are an admin of every plant automatically.
Assign a person to a plant
They must already have clicked “Request an account” on the login page. Assigning someone who already has a role in that plant changes their role. To see or remove a plant’s people, use “People” on the plant above.
Super-admins
A super-admin can create and delete plants and is an admin of every plant. Keep this list short.
